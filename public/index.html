    Our Story

    “Out of Many, One People” — Jamaica's national motto

    Cooked the Jamaican way. Served with Maine heart.

    Jeff's Jamaican Cuisine is run by Jeff and Sophia Bailey. They bought their first food truck in 2020 and set up on Lisbon Street with one simple promise: real Jamaican food, done right — seasoned with local herbs, marinated in Jamaican sauce and spices, and cooked to perfection.

    Six seasons later there are two trucks, a loyal Lewiston-Auburn following, and lines at fairs and festivals all over the state — from Sugarloaf's Reggae Fest to Lewiston's RiverFest.
